# Company Policy Handbook

## Leave Policy

Full-time employees receive 24 paid leave days per calendar year. Unused leave can be carried forward into the next calendar year, but the maximum carry-forward balance is 10 days. Any unused leave above 10 days expires at the end of the year.

Part-time employees receive paid leave on a prorated basis according to their weekly working hours. Part-time employees can carry forward up to 5 unused leave days into the next calendar year.

Contractors are not eligible for paid leave and cannot carry forward unused leave days. Contractors may request unpaid time away from work, but the request must be approved by their project manager.

Employees should submit planned leave requests at least 10 working days before the start date. Emergency leave can be reported as soon as reasonably possible through the HR portal or by notifying the employee's manager.

## Remote Work Policy

Full-time and part-time employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval. The employee must remain reachable during core collaboration hours from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM in their local time zone.

Contractors may work remotely only if remote work is included in their contract or approved by the project manager. Contractors must follow the same security and confidentiality rules as employees when accessing company systems.

Employees who work remotely are responsible for maintaining a secure internet connection and a private working environment. Public Wi-Fi may not be used for accessing internal systems unless a company-approved VPN is active.

## Security Policy

All employees and contractors must use multi-factor authentication for company accounts. Passwords must not be reused across personal and company systems.

Company laptops must be locked when unattended. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to IT within 2 hours of discovery.

Sensitive customer information must not be copied into personal notes, public AI tools, or personal cloud storage. Customer data may only be stored in approved company systems.

## Expense Policy

Employees may claim reimbursement for approved business expenses such as travel, accommodation, meals during business trips, and required software subscriptions.

Meal reimbursement during business travel is capped at 1500 INR per day unless a higher amount is approved before the trip.

Contractors can claim expenses only if reimbursement is explicitly included in their contract or approved in writing before the expense is made.

All expense claims must include receipts and must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date.

## Equipment Policy

Full-time employees are eligible for a company laptop and standard accessories. Part-time employees may receive company equipment if required by their role.

Contractors are expected to use their own equipment unless the project contract states otherwise. If company equipment is issued to a contractor, it must be returned within 5 working days after the contract ends.

Employees leaving the company must return laptops, ID cards, access keys, and any other company assets before their final working day.
